# Make it yours

You're helping someone who isn't a designer turn a generic-looking app into something that feels like **their** brand. Avoid the default AI look (purple gradients, generic cards, the same rounded everything) unless they choose it.

## 1. Find the personality
Ask one question at a time, with tap-to-answer options:
- Three words for how the brand should feel (offer pairs to choose from: calm or bold, playful or serious, warm or sharp, premium or friendly).
- Who it's for, and two or three brands those people already love.
- Anything they already have: a logo, colours, fonts, a website. If they share it, extract the real colours and fonts from it.
- Anything they hate.

## 2. Propose a small design system
Show it before applying anything:
- **Colour:** 4–6 named colours (background, text, surface, primary accent, plus at most one more), with hex values and where each is used. Check text contrast is readable.
- **Type:** one or two typefaces (free ones, e.g. from Google Fonts), and a simple scale for headings and body.
- **Shape and space:** corner radius, spacing steps, shadow style. One choice each, used everywhere.
- **Voice:** three rules for how the copy sounds, with a before/after line.
Offer two contrasting directions if they're unsure, and let them pick or mix.

## 3. Apply it consistently
- Put the system in one place the whole app uses (design tokens or theme variables), not scattered values.
- Apply it page by page on a **branch**. Replace generic copy with copy in their voice.
- Make one distinctive choice per page memorable (a bold headline style, a signature colour moment), and keep everything else calm.

## 4. Show before and after
Take screenshots of the key pages before and after, side by side, and explain the changes in plain English.

## 5. Write it down
Save a one-page brand guide (colours, type, spacing, voice rules, do's and don'ts) and add a short "Design" section to CLAUDE.md so every future change keeps the look consistent.
